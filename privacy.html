Privacy notice

Last updated November 27, 2026

Headwaters Advisory Group LLC ("Headwaters", "we") runs this website. This notice explains what information we collect through it and what we do with it. The short version: we collect only what you choose to send us, we use it to reply, and we never sell it.

What we collect

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive what you provide: your name, email address, organization (if you include it), and your message.

We don't use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies of our own on this site.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message and, if you'd like, set up a conversation.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence with you.

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information, and we don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Services that help run the site

A few providers handle parts of the site on our behalf:

  • Netlify hosts the site and receives contact form submissions, which it stores so we can read them.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on these pages. Your browser requests them from Google, which receives standard technical information such as your IP address.

Like most web hosts, these providers may keep basic server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested, for security and reliability.

How long we keep it

We keep contact messages only as long as we need them to respond and to maintain a reasonable record of our business conversations. Ask us at any time and we'll delete what you've sent.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information you've sent us by emailing {{EMAIL}}. We'll respond within 30 days.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we handle information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
